### 5. Component Recommendation (CTune)

### 6. Production and Fabrication
- **Trace Manufacturability**: 0.3mm width and 0.2mm spacing are robust for standard 1 oz copper processes; no yield issues expected.
- **Keep-Out Zone**: The 5mm element-centric keep-out must be strictly maintained on all PCB layers. Any copper (pours, traces, or ground planes) entering this 5mm zone will shift inductance and detune the tag.
- **Mounting Surface**: These results assume an air environment. If this tag is applied to plastic, wood, or glass, resonance will shift down. If mounted to metal, a ferrite absorber is mandatory and the coil must be completely redesigned.

### 7. Optimization Suggestions
This design is well-optimized.

### 8. Validation & Certification
These are calculated/simulated values, not measurements. A finished tag MUST be validated with hardware testing on the assembled inlay, on its final mounting surface.
- LCR/VNA: measure the bare coil's L and SRF; investigate any >10% deviation from the calculated values before proceeding.
- Assembled-tag resonance: measure with a loop-probe dip method or impedance analyzer with the chip populated; target slightly above 13.56 MHz per the tuning practice above.
- Functional test with the real target reader(s) at the intended mounting position - tag performance is only meaningful against a reader.
- Formal interoperability where required: ISO/IEC 10373-7 (ISO 15693) test methods or NFC Forum certification.
- A passive tag contains no transmitter, so its regulatory exposure is far lighter than a reader's - but the finished product still follows its normal compliance path, and in-situ verification on the final material is mandatory.
