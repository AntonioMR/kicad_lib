- Status: PASS (full margin).

### 5. Component Recommendation (CTune)
- STATUS: no external CTune required - the coil's native tune with the chip's internal 28.5 pF lands within tolerance. Do not add one.

### 6. Production and Fabrication
- **Trace Manufacturability**: The 0.3 mm width and 0.25 mm spacing are robust for standard low-cost PCB or flex-inlay production.
- **Keep-Out Zone (always mention this)**: The keepoutMode is elementcentric with a 5 mm distance and 5 mm ground clearance. This zone must stay copper-free on all layers; copper inside it (especially a pour) detunes the coil, shifting L, SRF, and the required CTune.
- **Mounting Surface**: These values assume an air environment. If the tag is applied to plastic, L will shift slightly; if applied to metal, a ferrite shielding sheet is mandatory and the design must be re-tuned.

### 7. Optimization Suggestions
This design is well-optimized.

### 8. Validation & Certification (MANDATORY)
These are calculated/simulated values, not measurements. A finished tag MUST be validated with hardware testing on the assembled inlay, on its final mounting surface.
- LCR/VNA: measure the bare coil's L and SRF; investigate any >10% deviation from the calculated values before proceeding.
- Assembled-tag resonance: measure with a loop-probe dip method or impedance analyzer with the chip populated; target slightly above 13.56 MHz per standard tuning practice.
- Functional test with the real target reader(s) at the intended mounting position - tag performance is only meaningful against a reader.
- Formal interoperability where required: ISO/IEC 10373-7 (ISO 15693) test methods.
- A passive tag contains no transmitter, so its regulatory exposure is far lighter than a reader's - but the finished product still follows its normal compliance path, and in-situ verification on the final material is mandatory.
